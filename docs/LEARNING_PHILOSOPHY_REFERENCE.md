# Diamond High — Learning Philosophy Reference

## Purpose and relationship
Scholar Build is the chosen pedagogical reference for Diamond High. This allows the project to build on existing thinking instead of repeating broad conceptual research. It is a reference for learning principles, not a product template. Diamond High remains independent and must validate its own activities, interfaces, and technical features.

## Principles to carry forward
1. **Understanding before memorisation:** connect recall to concepts, meaning, and use.
2. **Reasoning:** explain relationships, justify choices, identify assumptions, and solve problems.
3. **Application:** use knowledge in exercises, practical tasks, and unfamiliar contexts where appropriate.
4. **Evidence of competence:** use explanations, worked solutions, practical outputs, or other relevant demonstrations. Page completion or time spent alone is not mastery.
5. **Disciplined practice:** connect purposeful practice to learning objectives and useful feedback.
6. **Review and continuous improvement:** revisit gaps, correct misconceptions, and build on earlier learning without treating progress as a permanent label.

## Working learning cycle
**Understand → Reason → Apply → Demonstrate → Review → Progress**

This is a high-level design reference; individual courses may use a different sequence where appropriate.

## Design questions
- What should the learner understand?
- What reasoning should they practise?
- How will they apply the idea?
- What observable evidence demonstrates the outcome?
- What feedback helps them improve?
- What happens if understanding has not yet been demonstrated?
- What is the next meaningful learning step?

## What this reference does not establish
It does not require copying Scholar Build's interface, modules, content, or implementation. It does not validate every assessment or progress indicator, replace subject-matter review or learner feedback, or justify collecting sensitive learner data.

**Status:** Adopted as the pedagogical reference. Specific learning designs remain subject to review and evidence.
