# Diamond High — Long-Term Product Roadmap

This roadmap describes possible long-term evolution. It is separate from the [Prototype Roadmap](ROADMAP.md). These are strategic horizons, not deadlines, delivery promises, or approved specifications.

## Product north star
Help learners move through: **Discover → Understand → Reason → Practise → Demonstrate → Review → Progress**.

## Horizon 1 — Core learning experience
Potential capabilities: course and subject discovery; learning paths and objectives; structured lessons; practice activities; explanatory feedback and retry/review; learner home with current learning and next steps.

## Horizon 2 — Evidence and learner development
Potential capabilities: learning history; competency/mastery indicators tied to explicit criteria; progress visualisations; identification of learning gaps; review tools. Indicators must not claim more than the evidence supports or reduce a learner to a score.

## Horizon 3 — Educator and course development tools
Potential capabilities: educator workspaces; course and lesson authoring; activity/assessment creation; feedback workflows; cohort insights with appropriate privacy and access controls.

## Horizon 4 — Personalised learning support
Potential capabilities: adaptive practice; review suggestions; recommendations grounded in demonstrated needs; AI-assisted explanations or tutoring with clear limitations and human oversight. AI should support, not replace, learner thinking.

## Horizon 5 — Institutional integration
Potential capabilities: integration with an existing school's courses, educators, and learners; institutional administration; appropriate identity, access, privacy, safeguarding, and governance; multi-institution support if justified. This future possibility does not change the current e-school identity.

## Cross-cutting requirements
Accessibility, inclusion, privacy, data minimisation, security, age-appropriate safeguarding, meaningful evidence, transparent limitations, maintainable engineering, testing, and feedback from learners and educators.

## Prioritisation
For every feature ask: Which learner problem does it solve? Which learning principle does it support? What evidence would show it works? What data and safety obligations does it introduce? Is it appropriate for the current horizon?

**Status:** Strategic direction only. The first prototype is governed by [Prototype Scope](PROTOTYPE_SCOPE.md).
