# Diamond High — Founding Charter

## 1. Purpose and identity

Diamond High is an **e-school: an online learning platform** built around a simple conviction: learning should be rigorous, supportive, evidence-informed, and human. Its purpose is to help learners develop understanding, reason through ideas, apply knowledge, demonstrate competence, and make meaningful progress.

The project is also a practical environment in which its creator learns software development by designing, building, testing, and improving a real product.

Diamond High may later be integrated with a real school or support school-affiliated learning programmes. That possibility does not change its present identity as an e-school.

## 2. Educational commitments

### Understanding before memorisation
Remembering information can be useful, but recall alone does not establish deep understanding. Learning experiences should help learners explain concepts, connect ideas, reason, and use knowledge appropriately.

### Reasoning and application
Learners should have opportunities to work through problems, justify decisions, apply ideas, and transfer understanding to unfamiliar situations.

### Evidence before mastery claims
Completion, attendance, time spent, or confidence alone should not be treated as proof of mastery. Claims about competence should be supported by relevant demonstrations of learning.

### Struggle calls for support
A learner who is stuck should receive explanation, useful practice, feedback, and a reasonable opportunity to try again. Difficulty is information about what support may be needed, not a permanent label.

### Questions and mistakes are welcome
Learners should be able to ask questions, challenge ideas respectfully, try approaches, and admit confusion without humiliation.

### High standards should be humane
The platform should preserve meaningful expectations while avoiding fear, shame, or punishment as substitutes for teaching.

### Progress should be transparent
Learners should be able to understand what they have practised, what they can currently demonstrate, where uncertainty remains, and what a sensible next step might be.

## 3. Pedagogical reference

Scholar Build is the project's chosen pedagogical reference. Its principles—understanding, reasoning, evidence, application, disciplined practice, and mastery-aware progression—can inform Diamond High's learning design.

Diamond High is not a copy of Scholar Build. It remains an independent product with its own audience, information architecture, interface, feature set, and technical decisions. The reference should reduce duplicated conceptual research, not replace validation of unfamiliar features or technical questions.

See [Learning Philosophy Reference](LEARNING_PHILOSOPHY_REFERENCE.md) for the practical translation.

## 4. Product and design commitments

- Make the online learning experience the centre of the product.
- Help learners discover learning paths, study lessons, practise, receive feedback, and understand progress.
- Use visual design to support comprehension, orientation, and motivation rather than decoration alone.
- Make accessibility, responsive layouts, and understandable interactions part of quality.
- Treat progress information as useful feedback, not as a way to reduce a person to a score.
- Avoid unsupported claims about educational outcomes or institutional accreditation.
- Consider future integration with real schools only when requirements and safeguards are clear.

## 5. Engineering and learning commitments

- Build in small, understandable increments.
- Prefer clear foundations over rushed complexity.
- Explain and document important decisions.
- Test behaviour instead of assuming that code works.
- Learn from errors and revise decisions when evidence supports doing so.
- Distinguish what exists now from what is only planned.
- Protect private information and avoid collecting sensitive learner data unless a clear, justified need and appropriate safeguards exist.
- Use fictional or sample learner data in the prototype.

## 6. What this charter does not claim

The initial project is a prototype and a learning project, not a production-ready learning management system, accredited institution, or validated educational intervention. The use of Scholar Build as a reference does not establish that every proposed Diamond High feature is pedagogically validated. Future AI features, advanced analytics, school integration, and multi-school support remain possibilities until designed, tested, and justified.

## 7. How the charter evolves

This charter is a starting point, not an untouchable rulebook. Changes should be made deliberately when project experience, learner feedback, or better reasoning supports them. Important changes should be recorded in the decision log.
